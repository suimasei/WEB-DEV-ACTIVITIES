Studio Album

love is not dying

"an album about anxiety, love, and growing up."

Released in 2020, this masterpiece by Jeremy Zucker serves as an intimate, poignant exploration of vulnerability, featuring standout tracks like "always, i'll care", "not ur friend", and "julia".

Tracklist

01. still

The ambient, raw cinematic intro that sets the heartbreaking mood of the album.

02. we're fucked, it's fine

A beautiful bittersweet song tackling overthinking and modern anxieties.

03. somebody loves you

A soft reminder wrapped in delicate acoustic tones and light melodies.

04. orchid

An underrated interlude style track filled with deep emotional weight.

05. lakehouse

An explosive production shift featuring heavy vocoders and swelling beats.

06. good for her

A short, poignant track about letting go and wishing someone the best.

07. not ur friend

An upbeat, sassy anthem about cutting off toxic people from your life.

08. full stop

An intense, multi-layered experimental piece with a massive beat drop.

09. julia

A beautiful cinematic climax driven by heavy synthesizers and raw vocals.

10. hell or flying

A vulnerable track exploring the terrifying balancing act of falling in love.

11. always, i'll care

A comforting, acoustic-led masterpiece dedicated to unconditional love.

12. brooks

A brilliant short instrumental interlude that acts as a deep breath.

13. oh, mexico

The perfect acoustic escapism track closing out the album with warm guitar plucks.
